# AXA and Bangkok Insurance pilot

Checked: 2026-08-15. Scope: public official insurer pages only. No quote flow,
personal data, or agent-site material was used.

## AXA Thailand

AXA's [official health page](https://www.axa.co.th/en/personal/health-insurance)
lists SmartCare Essential, SmartCare Value, EasyCare Visa, Smart Health for
SMEs, SwitchCare, and International Exclusive. Smart Health for SMEs is group
cover and is outside the individual medical-expense comparison.

- [SmartCare Value](https://www.axa.co.th/th/personal/health-insurance/value-plan)
  publishes eligibility and benefit limits, but no complete public age-banded
  premium table.
- [EasyCare Visa](https://www.axa.co.th/th/personal/health-insurance/visa-plan)
  publishes eligibility, renewal and benefit information, including waiting
  periods in the terms summary, but not a complete age-banded premium table
  tied to filed wording.
- [SmartCare Cancer](https://www.axa.co.th/th/personal/health-insurance/cancer)
  is recorded separately as `cancer`; it is a lump-sum cancer candidate, not a
  medical-expense plan.
- [SwitchCare](https://www.axa.co.th/th/personal/health-insurance/global-plan)
  is quote-oriented with large annual limits; it is not entered without a
  premium source and matching filed wording.

Disposition: the two AXA coverage records are `insufficient_data`. AXA is a
good follow-up candidate, but entering a plan from the marketing pages would
turn quote-only or incomplete pricing into a false comparison.

## Bangkok Insurance

Bangkok Insurance's [official health product page](https://www.bangkokinsurance.com/th/product/health/new)
lists Classic Care, Superior Care, and Premier Care. Its [official register](https://www.bangkokinsurance.com/product/health/new/register?care_name=CASUPERIOR)
publishes age bands and annual premium tables with and without OPD. The official
[Superior selling kit](https://www.bangkokinsurance.com/download/AIS/selling-kit/Selling%20kit%20สุขภาพ%20Superior.pdf)
also provides benefit and premium detail.

Disposition: Bangkok Insurance is the strongest next data-entry candidate. It
remains `insufficient_data` until the register/selling-kit version, premium
conditions, and filed wording are reconciled. The selling kit is useful evidence
but is not treated as filed policy wording.
